How it works

01
Decode
Your browser's Web Audio API converts the WAV into raw PCM samples.
02
Autocorrelate
Multiple overlapping windows are autocorrelated to find the repeating period of the waveform.
03
Vote
Each window casts a vote for the nearest note. The majority wins.
04
Display
The winning note, its frequency, MIDI number and tuning accuracy are shown instantly.
